Checked tones (7–9) are the tones of syllables ending in a stop (-p, -t, -k). Jyutping writes them with 1, 3, 6, and Dunghoi Jyutping reuses the unmarked a (tone 7 = tone 1) and macron (tone 9 = tone 4); in those cases the coda decides which tone is meant. Yale marks low tones with an h after the vowel (before any -m/-n/-ng/-p/-t/-k coda).

🔡 Initial Consonants

Highlighted cells differ between the systems. Q, R, V and X are not used in any of them.

🔠 Finals

— means the system has no standard spelling for that final (Yale has none for /ɛːu, ɛːm, ɛːp, œːt/; ILE has none for /œːt/). When converting into such a system, the Jyutping spelling is used and a warning is shown. Dunghoi Jyutping spells both /œː/ and /ɛːu/ as eu; when reading Dunghoi Jyutping, eu is taken to be /œː/. Syllables written jyu, jyun, jyut (Jyutping / ILE / Dunghoi) are the same as zero-initial yu, yun, yut.
